# bread
this makes a single loaf. scale accordingly

## starter maintainence
every day, around the same time, combine into a fresh jar:
- 100g starter (omit if first day)
- 50g flour
- 50g water

discard or use the rest of the unused starter.

## day before
prepare leaven by combining in a bowl:
- 100g starter
- 200g warm water
- 200g flour

let sit overnight. test readiness by placing a chunk of leaven into water and it should float.

## day of
combine:
- 100g starter
- 350g water
- 500g flour

rest 30min at room temp.


add:
- 10g salt
- splash of water

for next few hours, every 30min fold dough on itself.

shape, place into floured bread basket and let it ferment and rise. slower rise (in fridge) yields more sour flavor.

bake in covered dutch oven at 500F for 15 min. lower to 450 until bread is done.
